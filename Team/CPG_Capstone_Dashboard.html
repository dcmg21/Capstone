      Draft map + class logistics (from Sept 8)

      Draft 0 = Introduction  |  Draft 1 = Lit Review (per lecture; the syllabus rubric expects more, see above)  |  Draft 2 = Methods + early results  |  Draft 3 = Results, Discussion, Conclusion (her "first draft of the final paper").

      20 sources minimum across the whole paper (intro + lit review + discussion all count), with the lit review solid by Oct 18. We have 11. The paper's lit review is not an annotated bibliography: weave sources into theme-organized paragraphs, give background on the methods too so the reader sees why ours fits, and end the lit review with our hypothesis. Anything we cite gets an in-text citation and a reference entry; anything we read but never cite is left off entirely. If we find a fact in paper A that A credits to paper B, cite both.

      Reading fast: abstract to decide if it is worth it, then intro, then jump to methods and results. That is all a lit review needs.

      Citations: pick ONE in-text style (numeric or author-year) and never mix; reference list in APA. RefWorks is free with your SMU account and auto-builds the bibliography. Purdue OWL for APA. Grammarly is allowed, even when it rewrites sentences. AI may be used to find literature and trace citations, not to write the paper.

      Audience: this is written for the SMU Data Science Review. The lecture said readers are data scientists, so skip basics like logistic regression; the syllabus says "students just beginning the MSDS program." DO explain the spirits/CPG domain they will not know. Past Data Science Review papers are the models she pulls from.

      Results: unremarkable results are fine. If it does not work, say so in the discussion and make the future-research section verbose.

      Submitting: share your Word doc with her directly (Word > Share). She does not send a SharePoint link. Required from Draft 1 on so she can comment inline. Only final presentations go to a Box link.

      Meetings: Draft 0 meeting optional; Drafts 1, 2, and 3 each require one. Draft 1 meeting is the week of Sept 28. Librarian Sylvia Jones guests the Sept 14 class and will meet with teams on request. Everything is due after class, never before.
